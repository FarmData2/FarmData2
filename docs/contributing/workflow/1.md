## 1. Find something to work on.

Here are a few suggestions for ways you might find something to work on in FarmData2.

- Discussing your skills, interests and ideas with a maintainer on the [Newcomers Zulip channel](https://farmdata2.zulipchat.com/#narrow/channel/603473-Newcomers).
- Look for [Good First Issues](https://github.com/FarmData2/FarmData2/labels/good%20first%20issue), [Good Second Issues](https://github.com/FarmData2/FarmData2/labels/Good%20Second%20Issue) or anything else of interest on the [FarmData2 Issue Tracker](https://github.com/FarmData2/FarmData2/issues).
- Propose something new for discussion on the [Suggestions Zulip channel](https://farmdata2.zulipchat.com/#narrow/channel/603478-Suggestions).
- Create a new issue on the [FarmData2 Issue Tracker](https://github.com/FarmData2/FarmData2/issues) for a bug you have discovered. Be sure to search the issue tracker first so that you do not create a duplicate issue.
