## 1. Find something to work on.

<!-- eslint enable no-heading-punctuation -->
<!-- vale RedHat.HeadingPunctuation = YES -->
<!-- vale Google.HeadingPunctuation = YEs -->
<!-- vale Microsoft.HeadingPunctuation = YES -->

Here are a few suggestions for ways you might find something to work on in FarmData2.

- Discuss your skills, interests and ideas with a maintainer on the [Newcomers Zulip channel](https://farmdata2.zulipchat.com/#narrow/channel/603473-Newcomers).
- Look for a ticket in the [FarmData2 Issue Tracker](https://github.com/FarmData2/FarmData2/issues). Tickets with the following labels are particularly good for getting started.
  - [![Good First Issue](../images/GoodFirstIssueLabel.jpg)](https://github.com/FarmData2/FarmData2/labels/good%20first%20issue) - The issues described in these tickets are the most approachable and do not require an in-depth knowledge of the project or its technologies. They typically involve changes that do not affect the application's behavior. Often a familiarity with Markdown, HTML, JavaScript or Bash scripting is what is necessary for addressing these issues. These issues provide a great way to get familiar with the workflow for making a contribution.
  - [![Good Second Issue](../images/GoodSecondIssueLabel.jpg)](https://github.com/FarmData2/FarmData2/labels/Good%20Second%20Issue) - The issues described in these tickets require some understanding of FarmData2 that is typically limited to one or two source files. They will often require changes that affect behavior and might also require creation or modification of unit, component or end-to-end tests. Thus, a deeper knowledge of Javascript, Vue.js, and Cypress tests will be necessary. These issues provide a great way to dig a little deeper, though still without requiring too deep a knowledge of the project.
- Create a new ticket on the [FarmData2 Issue Tracker](https://github.com/FarmData2/FarmData2/issues) describing a bug you have discovered. Be sure to search the issue tracker first so that you do not create a duplicate ticket.
- Propose something new for discussion on the [Suggestions Zulip channel](https://farmdata2.zulipchat.com/#narrow/channel/603478-Suggestions). Do not create new tickets for ideas that have not first been discussed with the community.

Note: the term _issue_ is used to describe something to be worked on. For example a bug or a feature. The term _ticket_ is used when talking about the entry in the issue tracker that describes the issue.
