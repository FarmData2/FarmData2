## 12. Address all comments, questions, and suggestions.

When you submit a pull request you will receive suggestions and feedback on your changes. Responding to the suggestions and feedback that you receive on your pull requests is essential to having your changes merged into FarmData2. Whether the feedback comes from [a Copilot code review](10.md) or a human FarmData2 maintainer the expectation is that you **carefully consider and respond to every comment and suggestion**.

- You might respond by:
  - agreeing with the suggestion, replying "I agree." and applying the suggestion as given.
  - disagreeing with the suggestion and explaining why you do not think it should be applied.
  - asking a follow up question about the suggestion.
  - giving an alternative change that you believe is better.
  - replying in any number of other ways that might lead to improvement of the pull request.

After you have responded to the feedback:

1. [Commit the changes to your feature branch](6.md).
2. [Push your feature branch to your fork on GitHub](8.md).
3. [Mark your pull request as ready for review](https://docs.github.com/en/pull-requests/collaborating-with-pull-requests/proposing-changes-to-your-work-with-pull-requests/changing-the-stage-of-a-pull-request#marking-a-pull-request-as-ready-for-review). The maintainer will have marked your pull request as _draft_, marking it as ready for review indicates that you have addressed the feedback.
4. [Mention (_tag_)](https://docs.github.com/en/get-started/writing-on-github/getting-started-with-writing-and-formatting-on-github/basic-writing-and-formatting-syntax#mentioning-people-and-teams) the maintainer who reviewed your pull request in a comment. This will notify them that you have reviewed and responded to the feedback, and that your pull request is ready for another review.

Taking a pull request from its initial form to being ready to be merged might require several rounds of feedback and revision. Responding to each round of feedback quickly will keep your work fresh in the maintainer's mind between reviews and improve the overall project efficiency.
