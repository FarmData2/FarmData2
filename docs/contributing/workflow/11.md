## Address all comments, questions, and suggestions. Commit, push and repeat.

When you submit a pull request you will receive suggestions and feedback on your changes. Responding to the suggestions and feedback that you receive on your pull requests is essential to having your changes merged into FarmData2. Whether the feedback comes from [a Copilot code review](10.md) or a human FarmData2 maintainer the expectation is that you **carefully consider and respond to every comment and suggestion**.

- You might respond by:
  - agreeing with the suggestion, replying "I agree." and applying the suggestion as given.
  - asking a follow up question.
  - explaining why you do not think the suggestion should be applied.
  - giving an alternative change that you believe is better.
  - reply in any number of other ways.
