# FarmData2 AI Policy

FarmData2 is at its core an application created for humans by humans. Working with people is what we find enjoyable about this project. We also recognize that AI tools can help us to produce a better product more quickly. This policy aims to provide guidance (and even encourage) the use of AI in FarmData2 while protecting the humanness of the community.

This policy is modeled on the structure of the [Fedora Council AI-Assisted Contributions Policy](https://docs.fedoraproject.org/en-US/council/policy/ai-contribution-policy/). The content of that policy has been adapted to FarmData2 with some details being adopted from or influenced by the following:

- [ASF Generative Tooling Guidance](https://www.apache.org/legal/generative-tooling.html)
- [Linux kernel AI Coding Assistants document](https://docs.kernel.org/process/coding-assistants.html)
- [LLVM AI Tool Use Policy](https://llvm.org/docs/AIToolPolicy.html)
- [The OpenInfra Foundation Policy for AI Generated Content](https://openinfra.org/legal/ai-policy)

1. You MAY use AI tools when contributing to or maintaining FarmData2 if you follow the principles described below.

2. **Authorship**: FarmData2 is [licensed](./LICENSE.md) under the GNU General Public License v3 ([GPL-3.0](https://www.gnu.org/licenses/gpl-3.0.md)) and the Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International License ([CC-BY-SA-4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/)) both of which require that contributions are [copyrightable](https://www.copyright.gov/comp3/chap300/ch300-copyrightable-authorship.pdf) to create their [copyleft](https://www.gnu.org/licenses/copyleft.en.html) requirements. To date "U.S. courts … have not recognized copyright in works that lack a human author-including works created autonomously by AI systems." [July 2025](https://www.congress.gov/crs_external_products/LSB/PDF/LSB10922/LSB10922.10.pdf) But "material originally generated by AI technology [can be modified by a human] to such a degree that the modifications meet the standard for copyright protection." [March 2023](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2023-03-16/pdf/2023-05321.pdf) As such, contributions to FarmData2 MUST show human engagement with any AI generated content in ways (modification, creative selection, coordination, or arrangement) that give a reasonable expectation that the contribution meets the standards for authorship and copyright protection.

3. **Accountability**: You MUST take the responsibility for your contribution. Contributing to FarmData2 means vouching for the authorship, quality, license compliance, utility of your submission, and respecting the time and effort of all members of the FarmData2 community. The contributor is always the author and is fully accountable for the entirety of these contributions. This accountability MUST be signified by the human author by attesting to the [Developer Certificate of Origin](https://developercertificate.org/) as described in [LICENSE.md](./LICENSE.md).

4. **Transparency**: You MUST disclose the use of AI tools when they have played a significant role in the creation of a contribution. Routine use of assistive tools for correcting grammar and spelling, clarifying language, adding small segments of code with autocomplete does not require disclosure. If in doubt disclose the use of the AI tools. See [CONTRIBUTING.md](./CONTRIBUTING.md) for details on how to disclose AI use in FarmData2 contributions.

5. **Contribution and Community Evaluation**: As a maintainer you MAY use AI tools to provide analysis, suggestions, and automated feedback to contributors. But maintainers MUST NOT use AI as the sole or final arbiter in making a final decision to accept or decline a contribution, nor to assess a person's standing within the community (for example for funding, leadership roles, or Code of Conduct matters). The responsibility for accepting or rejecting a contribution, even if implemented by an automated system, always rests with the human maintainer who authorizes the action.

Maintainers MAY decline to review (or end the review of) a contribution based on this policy if in their judgment it does not:

- meet the authorship and transparency requirements described above.
- give reasonable confidence that the author has reviewed and understands any AI generated content in the contribution.
- show that the author will be willing and able to answer questions and respond to feedback without reliance on AI tools.

When declining or terminating a review based on this policy maintainers SHOULD provide an explanation for the decision, which MAY minimally be a referral to this policy.

6. **Concerns**: Concerns about possible violations of this policy SHOULD be reported via the contacts contained in the [Enforcement Responsibilities section](./CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md#enforcement-responsibilities) of the FarmData2 Code of Conduct.
